[warmly] Hi, this is South Signal Lab. My name is Axel, and today I’ll show you Divider Bank — the fastest way to turn one timing source into eight related clock divisions without configuring eight individual channels one by one. [short pause]

Press the encoder to open the overview. Hold TAP and turn one detent to Divider Bank. Release TAP, move the confirmation from NO to YES and press the encoder. Instead of treating the outputs as eight separately edited channel configurations, Divider Bank assigns a coordinated family of divisions across all eight physical outputs. The screen now reflects the selected bank rather than an individual Clock channel.

Press the encoder to edit the bank choice. The initial family uses powers of two, the familiar sequence of repeated halving. Turn one detent to the integer family and press again to confirm. The available bank choices also include a prime-number family for different rhythmic relationships. We are naming that option here; this example leaves the integer bank selected.

[thoughtful] Return to the main screen and press PLAY. The scope provides a visible reference for one of the bank outputs while the divider set runs. Divider Bank is useful when you want conventional clock divisions quickly. All eight outputs remain derived from the same timing reference, so the divisions keep their relationship to the incoming master pulse. Press STOP when you have seen enough.
