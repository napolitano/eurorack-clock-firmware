[thoughtful] Even though this is an Independent channel, it is not a second free-running master clock. Its events are still derived from CLOCK’s common musical reference, which keeps different outputs musically related even when their rates and channel settings differ.

Press STOP slash BACK when you are finished and CLOCK returns to a defined stopped state.
