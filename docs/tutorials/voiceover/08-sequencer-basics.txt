[warmly] Hi, this is South Signal Lab. My name is Axel, and today I’ll show you the basic editing workflow in CLOCK’s Sequencer mode — selecting the mode, moving around the step grid, toggling a gate, and then watching the result run. [short pause]

Select Sequencer from the mode carousel. Unlike Euclidean mode, Sequencer lets you decide directly which individual steps produce gates instead of calculating the distribution from a hit count.

Move to step two with the encoder and press the encoder to toggle that gate. Then press PLAY and the scope shows the resulting gate sequence.

[thoughtful] Patterns are not limited to one sixteen-step page. Sequencer supports longer patterns, and the display follows the active block so you can edit them without squeezing every step onto the OLED at once.

The Sequencer defines event placement, while CLOCK’s shared master timing still defines when those steps occur.
