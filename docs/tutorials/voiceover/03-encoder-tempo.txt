On the performance screen, turn clockwise and the BPM goes up. Turn the other way and it comes back down. No menu, no edit mode. You can see the value change while your hand is still on the encoder.

That number is the master tempo. The channels can divide it, multiply it, or make patterns from it, but they’re still working from the same musical reference. When I change the BPM here, those relationships move with it.

That’s useful if you have several patches running. I can speed the whole thing up without visiting eight channel menus. Each output keeps its own rate or pattern; I’ve just moved the tempo they share.
