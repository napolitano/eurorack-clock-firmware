We start in STOP. One press of PLAY gets things moving. Press PLAY again and it pauses. Press it a third time and CLOCK resumes. It’s the same button, but what it does depends on whether the transport is running or paused.

Now press STOP slash BACK. [slowly] This one is different: it stops the run and resets the global phase. The next start has a defined beginning. You don’t have to guess where the different outputs will pick up again.

There’s one other case worth knowing. Even if an external clock is connected to SYNC, a manual STOP stays in charge. The incoming pulses can still be there; CLOCK won’t quietly restart just because it sees another one.

So: PLAY starts, PLAY again pauses, and PLAY resumes. STOP ends the run and resets phase. Once you’ve got that distinction, the front-panel transport is pretty straightforward.
